This panel shows what the proof proves: Bank B accepts the packet only when a storage proof matches a packet commitment under a trusted Bank A state root. The backend relays data, but it does not get to decide whether the voucher is minted.

No proof imported yet

Lock collateral or burn voucher first; then Linky will point to the trusted header and storage proof path.

1 Packet committed Bank A writes the packet commitment.
2 Header fetched Controller reads the relevant Besu header.
3 Light client updated Destination imports the trusted state root.
4 Storage proof generated Packet path is proven under the state root.
5 Packet verified Contract executes the receive or unlock.
6 Receipt consumed once Packet receipt prevents duplicate execution.
7 Replay attack rejected Shown only after an explicit duplicate proof attempt.
8 Timeout/refund verified Receipt absence proves refund eligibility.
9 Client frozen/recovered Safety controls handle conflicting evidence.
Thesis meaning

Proof-checked collateral representation lets the lending pool accept cross-chain collateral without trusting the relayer as a balance oracle.
